ARTIPHACT
=========

You plugged in the panel. Windows should show this as a USB drive.

1. Double-click  SETUP.BAT
2. Allow the install
3. Setup prints your panel SERIAL (it never changes, even on another PC)
4. Leave Artiphact open when the panel is plugged in
5. Restart Claude or ChatGPT once
6. Talk to your AI, e.g. "Keep NFL scores up to date on my Artiphact panel"

No Cursor required. Artiphact handles the PC side.
